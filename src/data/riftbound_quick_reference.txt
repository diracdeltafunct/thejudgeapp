## Start of Game Procedure
@cr: 110
1. Champion in legend zone
2. Choose Chosen Champion
3. Choose Battlefields
4. Shuffle and present both decks
5. Determine turn order
6. Draw 4
7. Mulligan up to 2 (recycle to bottom, then draw that many)
8. Begin Play

## Start of Turn
@cr: 315
1. Awaken
    - Ready all game objects controlled by turn player
2. Beginning
    - Start of beginning effects/triggers
    - Holding occurs
3. Channel
    - Turn player channels 2 runes.
    - Do any channel effects
4. Draw
    - Turn player draws 1 card
    - Do any effects
    - Rune pool empties

## Banned List
Cards:

- Called Shot
- Draven, Vanquisher
- Fight or Flight
- Scrapheap
- Stealthy Pursuer

Battlefields:

- The Arena's Greatest
- Aspirant's Climb
- Dreaming Tree
- Obelisk of Power
- Reaver's Row
